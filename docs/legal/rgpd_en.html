What this template already does about cookies

This application stores a little information on each visitor's device — the session that keeps them signed in, the token that protects forms, the language they chose, the record of their answer to the storage notice, and a few keys the interface needs to draw a page. All of it is strictly necessary to deliver the service the visitor asked for, so it is exempt from consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, and the notice shipped on the first visit therefore informs rather than asking. The inventory published at /cookies is yours: it comes from manage.py seed_cookies, and it names your deployment as the provider.

If you add anything outside that exemption — audience measurement, advertising, a social network widget, a third-party script of any kind — three things become your responsibility, and only the first is automatic. Declare it in seed_cookies with the category it belongs to: the notice will then ask for consent, offer refusal as easily as acceptance, and delete what was refused, with no further work. Then say so in this document. And do not leave the published page claiming you track nobody — that sentence is already written to disappear by itself as soon as your inventory contradicts it.
